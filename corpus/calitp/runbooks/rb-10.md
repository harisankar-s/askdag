## When to use

Use this when the uniqueness test on operator and pay window in fct_payments_aggregations_enghouse fails, or Enghouse settled totals don't match their monthly statement. Most problems start in fct_payments_settlements_enghouse, when corrected settlements are kept alongside the originals.

## Steps

1. Find settlement IDs that appear more than once in fct_payments_settlements_enghouse, and check whether they differ by correction sequence number.
2. If they do, the dedup is keeping more than the latest correction; fix the key.
3. Rebuild fct_payments_settlements_enghouse, int_payments__settlements_to_aggregations_enghouse and fct_payments_aggregations_enghouse.
4. Compare the month's settled total per operator with the Enghouse statement. The difference should be under 0.1%.

## Check

- The uniqueness test on operator and pay window passes.
- int_payments__settlements_to_aggregations_enghouse has one row per operator and payment reference.
- Monthly totals match the statement.
