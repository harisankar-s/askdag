## When to use

Use this after any change to int_gtfs_quality__daily_assessment_candidate_entities, or when organizations or datasets suddenly appear in or disappear from quality reports, the monthly reports index or TIDES. That table decides which organization, service and dataset combinations exist on a given date, and more than one product reads it: idx_monthly_reports_site (which organizations get a monthly report), fct_tides_vehicle_locations through the provider data, and tides_provider_gtfs_data.

## Steps

1. Compare the candidate count per day for the last week. A drop or jump of more than 5% almost always means a filter change.
2. If the change was intended, tell the tides team and the reports owner before merging; both consume the table.
3. Rebuild the table and everything downstream in one run: `dbt build --select int_gtfs_quality__daily_assessment_candidate_entities+`.
4. If only TIDES is affected, you can limit the run to `+tides_provider_gtfs_data` and `+fct_tides_vehicle_locations`, but rebuild idx_monthly_reports_site before the first of the month either way.
5. Hold the TIDES export until its relationships tests pass.

## Check

- The relationships tests from tides_provider_gtfs_data to the publication organizations pass.
- idx_monthly_reports_site has the same organizations as last month, plus or minus known onboarding changes.
- fct_tides_vehicle_locations has a provider for every row.
