## When to use

Use this when a unique test fails in a TIDES reference model such as tides_gtfs_datasets. Most of these failures start four models upstream, in int_gtfs_quality__naive_organization_service_dataset_full_join, when a new relationship in Airtable is allowed by the data model but not by the key the join builds.

## Steps

1. Take one duplicated key from tides_gtfs_datasets and look it up in each model between it and the join: the provider data, the candidate entities, then the join.
2. The first model where the key is duplicated is where to fix it. Usually that's int_gtfs_quality__naive_organization_service_dataset_full_join.
3. Check the Airtable records for the duplicated dataset; a second service relationship is the most common cause.
4. Fix the key (include the relationship that's now allowed to repeat) and rebuild from the join down.
5. Release the TIDES export once its tests pass.

## Check

- The unique test on key in tides_gtfs_datasets passes.
- The row count of tides_gtfs_datasets changed only by the duplicates.
