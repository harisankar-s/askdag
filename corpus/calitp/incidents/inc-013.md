## Summary

On 3 August dim_county_geography had two current rows for 14 counties. Those counties are served by more than one transit service, and a change on 1 August added the service key to the dimension to support a new report. That turned a one-row-per-county table into one row per county and service, while the model kept its name and description.

## Impact

- Organization counts by county were inflated wherever reports joined through dim_county_geography.
- The district summary showed 71 counties in California.

## Root cause

Duplicate rows from a grain change in dim_county_geography. The new column belonged in a bridge table, not in the dimension.

## Fix

Removed the service key from dim_county_geography, moved the county-to-service mapping into its own bridge model, pointed the new report at the bridge, and rebuilt.

## Follow-ups

- Add a uniqueness test on the county key for current rows.
- The steps for finding and fixing a fan-out in a dimension are written up in rb-07.
