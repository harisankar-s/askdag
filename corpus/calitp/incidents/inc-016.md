## Summary

From 30 September, a handful of real-time feeds were checked against the wrong schedule. The mapping that pairs each real-time dataset with the schedule it should be validated against had a change that preferred the most recently created schedule dataset when an agency had two. For three agencies that had published a test schedule feed, the test feed won. The vehicle positions latency summaries for those feeds then reported odd results, because many messages could not be matched to a scheduled trip.

## Impact

- Validation notices for three agencies jumped from dozens a day to thousands.
- The daily latency summary for their vehicle positions feeds was not comparable with earlier days for 30 September to 2 October.

## Root cause

A logic bug in the mapping: "newest dataset" is not the same as "the dataset the agency marked as the one to use".

## Fix

Changed the mapping to use the dataset the agency marked for validation and to fall back to the newest only if none is marked. Rebuilt the mapping and backfilled the latency summaries for the three days.

## Follow-ups

- Add a check that each real-time dataset maps to exactly one schedule dataset per date.
- Ask the transit database team to flag test feeds explicitly.
