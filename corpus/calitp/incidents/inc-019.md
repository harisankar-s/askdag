## Summary

On 31 October the vehicle positions message counts per dataset jumped for some agencies and dropped to zero for others. A change to dim_gtfs_datasets had made base64_url the unique join key for current datasets, but four agencies share one URL with a regional feed. fct_vehicle_positions_messages joins messages to datasets on that URL and the current flag, so the messages were attributed to whichever dataset sorted first.

## Impact

- Message counts per dataset in fct_vehicle_positions_messages were wrong for four agencies and one regional feed from 30 October.
- RT completeness scores for those agencies were wrong in the weekly quality report.

## Root cause

A logic bug: the dimension's change assumed URLs are unique per dataset, and they are not for regional feeds.

## Fix

Joined on the dataset key from the URL-to-dataset mapping instead of on the URL alone, rebuilt dim_gtfs_datasets, and re-ran the RT quality reports for the week.

## Follow-ups

- Document in dim_gtfs_datasets that base64_url is not unique.
- Add a test that no current URL maps to more than one dataset unless it is flagged as regional.
