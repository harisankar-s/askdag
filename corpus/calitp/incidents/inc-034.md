## Summary

On 21 August the GTFS datasets sync returned an empty extract again. The row-count guard added after April caught it and failed the sync, but the scheduler then ran the next job anyway, which read the previous partition of airtable.california_transit__gtfs_datasets with a filter on today's date and found nothing. bridge_schedule_dataset_for_validation and base_tdqi_gtfs_datasets_ct_gtfs_datasets_map, which maps the datasets table in the California Transit base to its copy in the data quality issues base, were both empty.

## Impact

- bridge_schedule_dataset_for_validation was empty for 21 August; RT validation was skipped for the day.
- base_tdqi_gtfs_datasets_ct_gtfs_datasets_map was empty, so new data quality issues could not be linked to datasets.

## Root cause

A source outage, plus a scheduler that didn't stop downstream jobs when the sync failed.

## Fix

Re-ran the sync once Airtable recovered, then rebuilt both models and re-ran RT validation. Made the downstream jobs depend on the sync succeeding.

## Follow-ups

- rb-02 updated: check that the sync succeeded before rebuilding, not only that it ran.
