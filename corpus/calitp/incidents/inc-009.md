## Summary

Elavon started sending transaction dates as YYYY-MM-DD instead of MM/DD/YYYY in their 10 May file. Our parse of elavon_external_tables.transactions expected the old format and returned null dates. The deposits fact carried the nulls forward, and fct_payments_aggregations could not work out the end-of-month date for any aggregation that included those deposits.

## Impact

- The not-null test on end_of_month_date_utc in fct_payments_aggregations failed on 11 May.
- 2,300 aggregations had no month, so they were left out of the monthly reconciliation view.

## Root cause

An upstream schema change: same column, new format. The parse used a single fixed format and gave null instead of an error on a mismatch.

## Fix

Changed the parse to accept both formats, re-ran the Elavon staging model for files from 10 May onward, and rebuilt down to fct_payments_aggregations. The test passed on the next run.

## Follow-ups

- Make the date parse fail loudly on values it can't read, rather than null them.
- Ask Elavon for a change notice before format changes; they agreed to email the payments team.
