## Summary

On 13 October an analyst found 52 feeds appearing twice in dim_schedule_feeds for 12 October. The parse job had been retried after a timeout, and the retry wrote a second outcome record for every file it re-parsed. stg_gtfs_schedule__file_parse_outcomes passes outcome records through one-to-one, so the feed outcome models saw two outcomes per file and produced two rows per feed.

## Impact

- 52 feeds had two rows in dim_schedule_feeds for one day.
- Daily trip counts for those feeds were doubled in the schedule marts until the rebuild.

## Root cause

Duplicate rows that started in the staging model: it assumed one outcome per file and parse date, and a retry broke that assumption.

## Fix

Added a dedup in stg_gtfs_schedule__file_parse_outcomes that keeps the latest outcome per file, feed and date. Rebuilt down to dim_schedule_feeds and the daily marts for 12–13 October.

## Follow-ups

- Add a uniqueness test on file, feed and date in the parse outcomes staging model.
- Make the parse job's retry overwrite rather than append.
