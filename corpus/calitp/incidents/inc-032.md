## Summary

On 18 June about 60 feeds were missing from dim_schedule_feeds. external_gtfs_schedule.fare_rules_txt_parse_outcomes had started writing an outcome record for feeds that have no fare_rules.txt at all, with `parse_success` false and the exception "file not present", after a parser change meant to make missing files visible. The grouped outcome models count any failed outcome as a failed feed, so feeds without fare rules (an optional file) dropped out.

## Impact

- About 60 feeds were missing from dim_schedule_feeds for 17–18 June, mostly small agencies without fare data.
- Their trips and stops were missing from the daily marts for the two days.

## Root cause

A schema change in the meaning of the outcome records: "file not present" became a failure row instead of no row.

## Fix

Treated "file not present" as not applicable for optional files in the grouping, rebuilt from the parse outcomes staging model down to dim_schedule_feeds for the two days.

## Follow-ups

- Keep a list of required and optional GTFS files in a seed and use it in the grouping.
- Add a test that no feed fails only because of optional files.
