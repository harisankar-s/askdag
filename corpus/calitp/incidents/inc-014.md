## Summary

On 22 August finance reported that the settlements table had "lost" settlements again, and since April's processor outage was still fresh, the first suspicion was another missing export. The files were all there. A change on 20 August to how the settlements fact picks the latest record for each settlement started keeping the first record instead of the latest. For settlements that were later reassigned to a different payment group, the kept record pointed at a group ID that no longer exists, so the relationships test between settlements and payment groups failed, and those settlements dropped out of the group summary.

## Impact

- About 1,200 settlements pointed at retired group IDs for two days.
- The monthly payment group summary undercounted settled amounts for August by about 2%.

## Root cause

A logic bug in the settlements fact: the dedup ordered by record time ascending instead of descending. No data was missing upstream.

## Fix

Fixed the ordering, rebuilt the settlements fact and the payment group summary, and checked settled totals per operator against the processor's monthly statement.

## Follow-ups

- Add a test that each settlement keeps its most recent record.
- Before re-pulling vendor files, check whether the raw files actually changed; that would have saved half a day here.
