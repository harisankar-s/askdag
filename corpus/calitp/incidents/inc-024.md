## Summary

On 10 January fct_daily_rt_feed_files showed no vehicle positions files for most URLs for 9 January. The external table external_gtfs_rt.vehicle_positions_outcomes had no partition for the 9th: the job that writes parse outcomes for vehicle positions ran out of memory on a day with unusually large files and restarted in a loop without writing anything.

## Impact

- fct_daily_rt_feed_files reported zero vehicle positions files for 9 January for about 90% of URLs.
- The RT feed health dashboard paged the on-call analyst, who confirmed the feeds themselves were fine.

## Root cause

A source outage in the vehicle positions parse outcomes. The files were fetched; only their outcome records were missing.

## Fix

Raised the job's memory limit, re-ran it for 9 January, refreshed the partition and re-ran fct_daily_rt_feed_files for the day.

## Follow-ups

- Alert when the parse job restarts more than three times in an hour.
- Add a check that fct_daily_rt_feed_files has at least one vehicle positions file for most URLs each day.
