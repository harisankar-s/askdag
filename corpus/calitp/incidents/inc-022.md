## Summary

From 10 to 17 December the download results for schedule zip files were not refreshed. The job that writes the day's download attempts had been moved to a new worker pool and lost write access to the bucket, so it logged successful downloads but never wrote the result records. The warehouse had no download results after the 9th. The feeds dimension treats a feed with no download record as not downloaded, so it stopped adding new feed versions, and every dataset kept its 9 December feed.

## Impact

- New feed versions published in that week were not in the warehouse for up to eight days, so schedule marts showed out-of-date service for agencies that had changed their schedules (several holiday schedules among them).
- Nobody was paged; an agency asked why their holiday schedule wasn't showing.

## Root cause

A source outage: the download results stopped arriving while the downloads themselves kept working. There was no freshness check on the download results.

## Fix

Restored write access, backfilled the download result records for 10–17 December from the job logs, and rebuilt the staged download results, the feed outcome models and the feeds dimension. The holiday schedules appeared that evening.

## Follow-ups

- Add a freshness check on download results (warn after 26 hours, error after 48).
- The backfill steps are in rb-06.
