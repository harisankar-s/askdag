## Summary

On 13 February fct_daily_rt_feed_files showed every service alerts file as failed. The parse job for service alerts had been upgraded, and the new version writes its outcome field as `success` instead of `parse_success`. external_gtfs_rt.service_alerts_outcomes still exposed the old name, which was now always null, and null counts as a failure.

## Impact

- fct_daily_rt_feed_files reported 0% successful service alerts parses for 12–13 February.
- The alerts feed health score for every agency dropped to zero on the dashboard.

## Root cause

An upstream schema change in the parse outcome records. The vehicle positions and trip updates jobs were not upgraded, so only alerts were affected.

## Fix

Mapped both field names in the service alerts staging model, re-ran it for 12–13 February and rebuilt fct_daily_rt_feed_files for the two days.

## Follow-ups

- Pin the outcome record schema across the three RT parse jobs and test it in the pipeline repo.
- Add a test that the daily success rate for each RT feed type stays above 50%.
