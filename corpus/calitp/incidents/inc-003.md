## Summary

On 18 March about a third of the day's schedule feeds were missing from dim_schedule_feeds. The parse job for agency.txt files crashed part-way through the run on a malformed file from one agency (a stray byte-order mark in the middle of the file), and the external table external_gtfs_schedule.agency only had rows for the feeds parsed before the crash. Feeds without an agency row were dropped when the dimension rebuilt.

## Impact

- 147 feeds had no row in dim_schedule_feeds for 18 March, so their trips, stops and routes for that date were missing from the daily marts.
- The service coverage dashboard showed a sharp dip for the date.

## Root cause

A source outage: one bad file stopped the whole parse job, instead of only the feed it belonged to. The dimension depends on agency rows to fill the feed's timezone, and treats a feed without one as incomplete.

## Fix

We skipped the bad file, re-ran the agency.txt parse for the date, then rebuilt dim_schedule_feeds and the daily schedule marts for 18 March. The agency was told about the stray byte-order mark and fixed their export.

## Follow-ups

- The parser now records a failed outcome for a bad file and continues with the rest.
- The rerun steps for a partial GTFS parse are written up in rb-05.
