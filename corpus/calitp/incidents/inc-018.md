## Summary

On 14 October the TIDES provider crosswalk was missing seven organizations that had been in the previous day's export. A change to the deduplicated vehicle locations model on 13 October mapped GTFS-RT vehicle status codes to text differently ("IN_TRANSIT_TO" became "In transit" instead of "In_transit_to"). The TIDES vehicle locations model checks that status against a fixed list, so the accepted-values test on current_status failed, the TIDES vehicle locations build stopped, and the publication models that list which organizations have data were built without the latest day.

## Impact

- Seven organizations were missing from the published crosswalk for one day.
- The accepted-values test on current_status in the TIDES vehicle locations model failed on every row from the affected feeds.

## Root cause

A logic bug in the upstream vehicle locations model: the text form of the status codes is part of a published contract and was changed without updating the TIDES side.

## Fix

Restored the original mapping, rebuilt the vehicle locations models and the TIDES publication models, and republished the crosswalk.

## Follow-ups

- Move the status code mapping into a seed shared by both sides.
- Mark the TIDES models as contract consumers in their descriptions so changes upstream get reviewed by the tides team.
