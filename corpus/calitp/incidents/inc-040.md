## Summary

On 24 October the relationships test from tides_provider_gtfs_data to the TIDES publication organizations failed for 41 rows. A change to int_gtfs_quality__daily_assessment_candidate_entities on 23 October included organizations whose status was "pending onboarding", which are not yet published in TIDES. The provider data and the TIDES crosswalk picked them up, so the crosswalk referred to organizations the publication list didn't have.

## Impact

- Rated sev1: the TIDES export runs to a fixed schedule for partner agencies, and it was held for 20 hours.
- No incorrect data was published.

## Root cause

A logic bug in int_gtfs_quality__daily_assessment_candidate_entities: a change to include more organizations in quality assessment had side effects in TIDES, which reads the same table.

## Fix

Reverted the change, rebuilt int_gtfs_quality__daily_assessment_candidate_entities, the provider data and tides_provider_gtfs_data, and released the export. The wider candidate list was reintroduced later behind a separate flag used only by quality assessment.

## Follow-ups

- Add the TIDES models to the review list for any change to the candidates table.
- rb-01 updated with the TIDES models.
