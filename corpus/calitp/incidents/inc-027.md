## Summary

On 16 February two unrelated-looking problems came in on the same morning: the monthly reports site had no report scheduled for eight organizations, and TIDES vehicle locations were missing their provider for the same organizations. Both trace back to the daily table of organization, service and dataset combinations that are candidates for quality assessment. A change on 14 February excluded any combination whose service was marked "seasonal", which those eight organizations use for summer and winter services.

## Impact

- The monthly report index left out eight organizations for February.
- TIDES vehicle locations for their services had no provider data for two days.

## Root cause

A logic bug in the assessment candidates model: a filter meant for retired services also caught seasonal ones.

## Fix

Narrowed the filter to retired services only, rebuilt the candidates table and the provider data, the TIDES vehicle locations and the monthly report index. Reports for the eight organizations were generated a day late.

## Follow-ups

- Add a check that the number of candidate organizations doesn't drop more than 5% in a day.
- The steps for rebuilding everything downstream of the candidates table are in rb-01.
