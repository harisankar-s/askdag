## Summary

On 8 June the routes.txt parse failed for two agencies, so external_gtfs_schedule.routes had no rows for their feeds that day. The routes dimension had no current version for those routes, and fct_payments_rides_v2 builds its route fields by joining each ride to the route that was valid on the ride's date. The branch for rides with no matching route left aggregation_id empty.

## Impact

- The not-null test on aggregation_id in fct_payments_rides_v2 failed, and the payments refresh stopped for the day.
- About 3,100 rides for the two agencies were held back until the next morning.

## Root cause

A source outage in the schedule data, showing up in a payments table. The rides model assumed every ride has a route version for its date.

## Fix

Re-ran the routes.txt parse for 8 June, rebuilt the routes dimension, and re-ran fct_payments_rides_v2. All rides matched a route.

## Follow-ups

- Change the rides join so a missing route keeps the ride and its aggregation_id, with null route fields and a warning flag.
- Tell the payments team that schedule outages can now reach their tables; the lineage view shows the path.
