## Summary

On 23 June about 120 feeds were missing from dim_schedule_feeds, which at first looked like a repeat of the March agency.txt outage. It wasn't: every file had parsed. A refactor of stg_gtfs_schedule__file_parse_outcomes, merged on 22 June, changed how parse_success was read from the outcome records, and outcomes written by the older parser version (still used for a subset of feeds) were read as failures. The feed outcome models then marked those feeds as not fully parsed, and the dimension left them out.

## Impact

- 118 feeds were missing from dim_schedule_feeds for 22–23 June, with the same downstream gaps in the daily schedule marts as in March.
- About two hours were spent re-running parses before anyone looked at the outcome records themselves.

## Root cause

A logic bug in the staging model, not a source problem. The older parser writes parse_success as the string "True"; the refactor compared it with a boolean, so it never matched.

## Fix

Cast the field properly for both parser versions, rebuilt stg_gtfs_schedule__file_parse_outcomes and the models between it and dim_schedule_feeds, and checked feed counts per day against the previous fortnight.

## Follow-ups

- Add a test that the share of successful parses per day stays above 90%.
- When feeds go missing from the dimension, check the parse outcome records before re-running any parse; the symptom is the same for very different causes.
