## Summary

On 31 March vehicle positions messages lost their schedule feed for about 25 agencies, the same symptom as in November. The unzip outcomes were fine this time. The download results job had a bucket permission change after a security review and wrote nothing for 30 March, so the feeds dimension had no new versions and, for agencies whose previous feed had expired that weekend, no current feed at all. Messages for those agencies could not be linked to a schedule.

## Impact

- Messages for 25 agencies had no schedule feed for 30–31 March.
- RT trip metrics for those agencies were missing for the two days.

## Root cause

A source outage in the schedule download results, which showed up in the real-time tables because they link messages to the current schedule feed.

## Fix

Restored the bucket permission, backfilled the download results for 30 March, and rebuilt the schedule feed models and the vehicle positions messages for the two days.

## Follow-ups

- The freshness check added in December warned on the 30th, but only in a channel nobody watches on weekends; it now pages.
- Backfill followed rb-06.
