## Summary

On 25 November fct_vehicle_positions_messages showed messages with no schedule feed for about 40 agencies, and RT completeness dropped. It looked like October's dataset attribution problem, but the dataset keys were right. The unzip step had written its outcome records to a new bucket prefix after a config change, and external_gtfs_schedule.unzip_outcomes still pointed at the old one, so it had no outcomes for 24–25 November. Without unzip outcomes the feed outcome models marked those feeds as never unzipped, dim_schedule_feeds had no current feed for them, and the messages had no schedule to link to.

## Impact

- About 30% of vehicle positions messages had no schedule feed for two days.
- Trip-level RT metrics were missing for those agencies in the daily marts.

## Root cause

A source outage: the external table's location was not updated with the pipeline config. The unzip itself had worked.

## Fix

Pointed external_gtfs_schedule.unzip_outcomes at the new prefix, refreshed it, and rebuilt the schedule feed models and fct_vehicle_positions_messages for the two days.

## Follow-ups

- Keep the bucket prefixes for external tables in one config file used by both the pipeline and dbt.
- Add a freshness check on unzip outcomes.
