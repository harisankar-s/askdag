## Summary

The Airtable services sync did not run on 11 April (the scheduler was paused during a maintenance window and not resumed). airtable.california_transit__services kept the previous day's extract, which was missing three services added on the 10th. tides_services was built without them, and vehicle location records for those services could not be matched to a service when the TIDES export ran.

## Impact

- The not-null test on event_timestamp in the TIDES vehicle locations fact failed, because records with no matching service also had no local timezone to convert from.
- The TIDES export for 12 April was held back for six hours.

## Root cause

A source outage: the sync silently did not happen. Nothing checks that the services extract is from today.

## Fix

Resumed the scheduler, ran the sync, and rebuilt from the services staging model down to tides_services and the TIDES vehicle locations. The export went out at 14:10.

## Follow-ups

- Add a freshness check on the services extract (warn after 26 hours).
- rb-04 now covers the TIDES rebuild as well as the bridge tables.
