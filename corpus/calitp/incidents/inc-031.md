## Summary

On 28 April the Airtable sync for the GTFS datasets table returned an empty extract: Airtable had a partial outage and answered with an empty page instead of an error. airtable.california_transit__gtfs_datasets had no rows for the day, so the datasets models had no current records and bridge_schedule_dataset_for_validation had no pairs. RT validation for the day ran against nothing.

## Impact

- bridge_schedule_dataset_for_validation was empty for 28 April.
- RT validation notices were missing for every agency for the day, and the RT quality scores showed a gap.

## Root cause

A source outage at Airtable, passed through because the sync accepts an empty extract.

## Fix

Re-ran the sync after Airtable recovered, rebuilt from the datasets staging model down to bridge_schedule_dataset_for_validation, and re-ran RT validation for 28 April.

## Follow-ups

- The services sync got a row-count guard after February 2025; extend it to the GTFS datasets sync.
- Steps are in rb-02.
