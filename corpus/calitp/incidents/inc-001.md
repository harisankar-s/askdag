## Summary

On the morning of 16 January the district filter on the organizations dashboard showed "(blank)" for 211 of 240 agencies. Someone on the transit database side had renamed the "Caltrans District" field in the county geography table in Airtable to "Caltrans District (number)" while cleaning up the base. The nightly extract kept running, but the renamed field arrived under a new key, so the staged county table had an empty district column and every organization joined to a county picked up nothing.

## Impact

- The current-organizations view used by the district dashboards had no district for about 88% of rows for one day.
- Two planners exported the list for a grant memo before the fix; both were told to re-export.
- No historical data was affected, because the county table only keeps the latest extract.

## Root cause

An upstream schema change. The extract maps Airtable field names to columns by name, and nothing warned when a field disappeared and another appeared. The staging model selects the old name, which came back null rather than failing.

## Fix

We renamed the field back in Airtable (agreed with the base owner), re-ran the extract, and rebuilt the county staging model and everything downstream of it. Districts were back by 11:40.

## Follow-ups

- Add a not-null test on the district column of the staged county table; it is described as required, but nothing checked it.
- Ask the transit database team to announce field renames in #data-help a day ahead.
