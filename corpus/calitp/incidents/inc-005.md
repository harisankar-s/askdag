## Summary

On 1 April the GTFS schedule status column in dim_services came back empty for every service. The transit database team had changed the "GTFS Schedule Status" field in airtable.california_transit__services from a single-select to a linked record, as part of a redesign. The extract now delivered a list of record IDs instead of a text value, and the staging model's cast turned it into null.

## Impact

- dim_services had no gtfs_schedule_status for about 1,900 services for one day.
- The quarterly "services without a schedule feed" list, which filters on that column, came out empty and was nearly sent to Caltrans as "all clear".

## Root cause

An upstream schema change: the field kept its name but changed its type. A not-null test on the column would have caught it, but the column is allowed to be null for services that are not yet assessed.

## Fix

Updated the services staging model to read the label of the linked record, rebuilt down to dim_services, and checked the counts by status against the previous week. The list was regenerated and sent a day late.

## Follow-ups

- Add a test that at least 80% of current services have a status.
- Ask the transit database team to tag schema changes in the base's changelog.
