## Summary

The Airtable sync for organizations stopped on 4 September when the Airtable base was moved to a new workspace and the base ID changed. The sync kept running against the old ID and got permission errors, which it logged as warnings. airtable.california_transit__organizations stayed on the 3 September extract, and dim_organizations stopped reflecting changes, including three new organizations added for a grant program.

## Impact

- dim_organizations was three days out of date.
- The three new organizations were missing from the grant program report, which was due on 7 September.

## Root cause

A source outage: the base ID changed and nothing failed.

## Fix

Updated the base ID, ran the sync, rebuilt from the organizations staging model down to dim_organizations, and re-ran the grant report.

## Follow-ups

- Treat permission errors in the sync as failures.
- The organizations recovery steps are in rb-03.
