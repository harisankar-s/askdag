## Summary

On 20 September the relationships test between rides and payment aggregations failed. The organizations sync had failed again (an expired token this time), and the extract for airtable.california_transit__organizations was empty for the day. dim_organizations had no current rows, and fct_payments_aggregations, which takes organization fields from it, dropped every aggregation whose organization could not be matched. Rides still pointed at those aggregation IDs, so the relationships test on fct_payments_rides_v2 failed.

## Impact

- The payments refresh stopped on the failed test; finance dashboards showed the previous day's figures.
- No wrong figures were published.

## Root cause

A source outage in the organizations sync, two weeks after the last one. The fix from inc-036 made permission errors fail, but an empty extract still passed.

## Fix

Rotated the token, re-ran the sync, rebuilt dim_organizations and fct_payments_aggregations, and the test passed.

## Follow-ups

- Add the row-count guard to the organizations sync.
- Followed rb-03; added the payments rebuild to it.
