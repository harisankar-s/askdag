## Summary

Settlements from Littlepay stopped landing in our bucket on 29 April after the processor rotated the credentials for the v3 export without telling us. The external table for the v3 settlements feed kept serving the last good file, so the staged settlements had nothing new, and the settlements fact for the month looked short to finance on the 30th.

## Impact

- About 9,400 settlements for 29–30 April were missing from the settlements fact for a day and a half.
- Month-end reconciliation for three operators was delayed.
- The not-null test on the staged settlements key failed on the 30th: a partial file that did land had empty key fields.

## Root cause

A source outage on the vendor side. Our export job logs a warning, not an error, when it can't authenticate, so the run looked green.

## Fix

Got new credentials from the processor, re-pulled 29 and 30 April, dropped the partial file, and rebuilt the settlements models down to the fact table. Finance re-ran reconciliation on 2 May.

## Follow-ups

- Make an auth failure in the export job an error.
- Ask the processor to include us in credential rotation notices.
