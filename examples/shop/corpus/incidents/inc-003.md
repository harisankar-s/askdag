## Summary

From 11 August, new customers in dim_customers had no region. The app team had changed the signup form to store three-letter country codes in app.customers, but the country_codes seed only has two-letter codes, so the join found nothing for 2,140 new accounts.

## Impact

- The growth team's regional signup report undercounted every region for nine days.
- Older customers were unaffected because their codes were already stored.

## Root cause

An upstream schema change. The country column in app.customers kept its name but changed format, so no test or type check noticed. The region column is documented as null when the country is unknown, which hid the problem.

## Fix

We added the three-letter codes to the country_codes seed and rebuilt dim_customers. The app team agreed to keep the old format in a separate column for a release.

## Follow-ups

- Add an accepted-values test on country_code length in the staging model.
- Watch the share of customers with a null region.
