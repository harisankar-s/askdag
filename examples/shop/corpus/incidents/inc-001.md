## Summary

The nightly replication of app.payments stopped at 02:10 on 4 March when the replica ran out of disk. Orders kept arriving, so fct_orders had 1,312 orders with no payment rows and a null amount. The not_null_fct_orders_amount test failed in the morning run, and mart_revenue_daily showed gross revenue about 40% below the day before.

## Impact

- The finance dashboard showed low revenue for 3 March until 10:30.
- One daily revenue email went out with the wrong total and was corrected by hand.

## Root cause

A source outage. The replica that copies app.payments filled its disk and stopped without alerting. The pipeline ran on the stale copy, and the left join in the order model turned missing payments into null amounts rather than failing.

## Fix

The platform team grew the replica disk and restarted replication. Once app.payments caught up, we rebuilt fct_orders and mart_revenue_daily. Totals matched the payment service by 10:30.

## Follow-ups

- Add a freshness check on app.payments so a stalled copy fails the run before the marts build.
- Alert on replica disk use above 80%.
