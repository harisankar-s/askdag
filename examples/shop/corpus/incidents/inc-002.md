## Summary

On 19 May mart_revenue_daily reported net revenue of $96,400 for the previous day, against $51,200 in the payment service. A payment service release had started writing a second success row when a card payment was retried. int_order_payments sums every successful payment per order, so retried orders were counted twice.

## Impact

- The finance dashboard overstated revenue for two days, 17 and 18 May.
- No customer was charged twice; only the reporting was wrong.

## Root cause

Duplicate rows. int_order_payments assumed at most one successful payment per order and summed them without checking. Nothing tested that assumption, so the duplicates passed every test.

## Fix

We changed int_order_payments to keep the latest successful payment per order, then rebuilt everything downstream of it. The payment service also stopped writing the extra row in a patch release on 20 May.

## Follow-ups

- Add a test that each order has at most one successful payment.
- Ask the payments team to announce changes to how attempts are recorded.
